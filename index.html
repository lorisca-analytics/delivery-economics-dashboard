The problem

One flat fee. Two wildly different deliveries.

The finding

Eight of thirteen customers lose money.

Cost allocated by volume — $0.2292 per cubic foot, stated not hidden. The five smallest orders subsidize the rest. A 20% routing improvement saves $96 against a $1,156 loss: 8% of the problem.

Per-customer P&L. The answer: volume-based pricing at $150 + $0.25/cu ft — or +$88.94 per delivery on flat fees.
